# Video & Voiceover Asset Pipeline

## Workflow
1. **Script Drafting**: Write clear, punchy narration with timestamp markers.
2. **Voiceover Generation**: Generate natural, professional audio narration with speech synthesis.
3. **Visual Composition**: Combine video background plates with branding overlays and synchronized subtitles.
4. **Quality Gates**: Measure audio loudness (-14 LUFS standard) and aspect ratio conformance with `ffprobe`.
