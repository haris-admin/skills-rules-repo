# Zero-Click Content Framework

## Principles
1. **Standalone Value**: Deliver the full insight, framework, or data directly in the post body.
2. **Native Formatting**: Optimize for platform-native consumption (bullet points, clear headers, concise takeaways).
3. **Selective Linking**: Place links only for users seeking deep technical documentation or source data.
