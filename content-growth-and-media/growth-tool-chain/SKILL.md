# Growth Tool Chain

## Research Chain
1. **Perplexity / Web Search**: Discover live authoritative sources, citations, and regulatory updates.
2. **Synthesis & Challenge**: Fact-check claims against primary legal/technical documentation.
3. **Actionable Artifact**: Draft structured guides, comparison tables, or feature briefs backed by dated evidence.
