# Multi-Channel Social Media Engine

## Channel Adaptations
- **LinkedIn**: Thought leadership, executive insights, clear paragraphs, professional tone.
- **X (Twitter)**: Concise single punchy takeaways or structured threads.
- **Reddit**: Deep, authentic, answer-first contributions adhering strictly to subreddit rules.
- **Facebook**: Approachable, community-centric explainers and product updates.
