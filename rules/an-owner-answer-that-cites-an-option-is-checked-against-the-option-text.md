# An owner answer that cites an option is checked against the option text

When a typed answer says "option 2" but describes something else, the description is the intent and the label is a slip, or the reverse. Do not pick one silently.

**Scope:** every structured question that allows free text.

---

## Core Directives

1. **Compare the typed description with the option text** before recording the answer.
2. **Ask one short clarifying question** that restates your reading and offers the matching option, and record both the original text and the clarification.
3. **Record free text verbatim** and put your reading in a separate, labelled line.
4. **Keep the unanswered details open** (who, when, how long) instead of filling them with a recommendation.

## Why this exists

**21 Nov 2026 (HandleDispute):** an answer chose "option 2" (keep the old contact details and claim by a code) while describing a flow where the case is assigned automatically to the requesting customer. A clarifying question showed the owner meant automatic assignment with the old details cleared.
