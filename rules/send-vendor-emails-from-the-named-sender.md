# Send vendor emails from the named sender

Draft vendor and partner emails in the voice and signature of the person Haris names as the sender, and never send them.

**Scope:** any email draft for a vendor, partner or customer written for Haris.

## Why this exists

Added 20 Oct 2026 (TapEase POS). Haris: "the email will be sent on behalf of Shoaib, not from Haris, so send it from Shoaib." The first draft to Clover was signed as Haris.

## Core Directives

1. Ask or check who sends the email before drafting; sign and write as that person.
2. Drafts stay drafts. Haris or the named sender sends them.
3. Put the evidence (device, versions, what was tried, log lines) in the email and the open questions as a lettered list.
4. No em dashes in drafted prose.
