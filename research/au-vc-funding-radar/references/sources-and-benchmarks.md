## Tracxn geography page — unstable, never quote

Snapshots of the same page across four months: "till June 2026 US$1.77B / 82 rounds" → "till July 2026
US$2.51B" → "till August 2026 US$4.57B / 119 rounds" → "till September 2026 US$4.66B / 133 rounds".
The jumps are impossible for a monthly series. Quote only the dated semi-annual report (H1 2026:
US$1.9B, +64%); a supplied "YTD Tracxn" number cannot be validated and must be flagged, not reused.
