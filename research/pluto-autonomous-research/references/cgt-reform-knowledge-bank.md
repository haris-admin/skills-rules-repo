## Key Data Points (for briefings, LinkedIn posts, blog content)

### Structural Weaknesses
- **2/3 of Australian startup funding** involves international investors (Scalare Partners data)
- **<0.5% of $4.3 trillion superannuation pool** reaches local VC/startups
- Australia is the **only country where the tech workforce is contracting** despite government ambition to reach 1.2M tech jobs by 2030
- **Fewer repeat founders** than peer ecosystems
- **Weaker follow-on funding** than US/UK/Israel
- **Less mature secondary markets** and slower liquidity cycles

### The Canva CR 2025/34 Tax Trap
- ATO ruled Canva's US restructuring (cancelling AU shares, replacing with US parent equity) was a vesting event
- Middle-income employees forced into **45% top tax bracket** on paper gains — no cash, no IPO
- Employees had to sell up to **67% of equity** to cover tax, or take company loans
- Demonstrates how ESS participants can face tax liabilities before genuine liquidity

### Angel Investing Economics (Cheryl Mack, Aussie Angels)
- The **indexation model** (proposed reform) evaluates each investment separately against inflation-adjusted cost base
- This breaks the portfolio model: under current 50% CGT discount, gains and losses wash out across a portfolio
- Angels build portfolios on power-law assumption: most fail, few carry the whole portfolio
- "We may find ourselves with no angel investors left" if indexation proceeds
- Example model: 4 × $10K investments over 10 years at 3.5% inflation → inflation-adjusted cost base grows 41% ($14,100 each). One 10x return, two flat, one bust. Under indexation: you're taxed based ONLY on the winner as if the losers didn't exist.

### Small Business CGT Concessions
- **$6 million net asset threshold** unchanged since 2005 (21 years)
- Sydney property prices increased 300-350% over same period → equivalent threshold would be $18-21M
- A startup can exceed $6M valuation while remaining capital-constrained, unprofitable, and highly risky

### Blackbird VC Reality Check
- Leading Australian VC Blackbird has invested billions in hundreds of companies
- **More than 2/3 of its returns will come solely from Canva's eventual liquidity**
- Innovation "consumes an excess of capital and energy, and doesn't deliver from a shareholder perspective" — the second mouse gets the cheese, but needs the first mouse

## 9 Recommendations from the Senate Submission

1. Additional protections for **employee share scheme participants** where tax events occur without genuine liquidity
2. Review treatment of **startup equity for middle-income earners**
3. Recognise **founder sweat equity** and entrepreneurial opportunity cost within the CGT framework
4. Create specific provisions for **qualifying angel investments**
5. Introduce **reinvestment/rollover relief** where proceeds are recycled into Australian startups and small businesses
6. **Modernise small business CGT concessions** — extend to non-ESIC startups, increase thresholds
7. Publish **Treasury modelling** on impact to: ESS, startup formation, founder retention, angel investment, domestic vs international ownership, capital recycling, innovation sector competitiveness
8. **Further consultation** with startup, VC, technology, and small business sectors before implementation
9. **Indexation model should not replace 50% CGT discount** for startup equity — the discount allows portfolio-level loss offsetting that indexation destroys
