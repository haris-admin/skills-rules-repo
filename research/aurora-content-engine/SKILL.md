# Aurora Content Engine

## Trigger
Any content task: blog post, compliance guide, LinkedIn post, SOV check, SEO/sitemap work.

## Method

1. **Pull fresh signals** from Mempalace (regulatory-ai, fintech-aml, payments-npp, startup-vc).
2. **Map to search intent**: what is the agency/compliance officer searching THIS week? (deadlines, fines, "Tranche 2 what do I do").
3. **Draft with structure**: hook → through-line → evidence → CTA.
4. **Humanize**: strip AI-isms (humanizer skill). Read it aloud — would a sharp human sign it?
5. **Guardrail check** (Caduceus rules):
   - ✅ sourced claims · ✅ entity firewall (no harishabib.au↔AMLHive link) · ✅ key line "no ID means no sale"
   - ❌ no "AUSTRAC-approved" · ❌ no "Compliant in 14 days" · ❌ no fabricated stats
6. **Save to content-drafts/** — NEVER auto-publish.

## Cadence
- 2–3 blog/week · 3–5 LinkedIn/week
- SOV check Friday 4:05 AM (fixed prompts vs ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini/Perplexity/Bing)

## SEO
- Sitemap sync: amlhive.com.au ↔ content bucket (check daily cron)
- IndexNow ping on publish
- Track Google/Bing visibility monthly

## Pitfalls
- Never publish without sign-off.
- Never link the two entities publicly.
- Honest baselines: a zero SOV baseline is data, not failure.
