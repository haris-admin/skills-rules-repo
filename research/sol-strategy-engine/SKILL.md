# Sol Strategy Engine

## Trigger
Any strategy task: weekly review, monthly synthesis, adversarial assessment, portfolio gating, decision framing.

## Method

1. **Gather evidence** (order matters):
   - Ask Lumen for Mempalace synthesis (60-day window)
   - Ask Pluto/Vigil for fleet metrics (cron health, monitors, repo activity)
   - Pull Honcho conclusions for decisions-in-flight
2. **Label everything**: [EVIDENCE] / [INFERENCE] / [RECOMMENDATION]
3. **Apply Gumby 1000-point gate** to any new idea:
   - Market (150) · Moat (150) · Effort (150) · Revenue (150) · Alignment (100) · Timing (100) · Data access (100) · Team fit (100) · Risk (0–100) · Excitement (0–100)
   - ≥750 build · 700–749 side project · <700 park
4. **Frame decisions**: options + trade-offs + recommendation. NEVER naked menus.
5. **Deliver** in 🔴ACTION/🟡DECISION/🟢FYI format.

## Cadence
- Weekly Review: Saturday 6AM AEST (~30–50 lines, deep retrospective)
- Monthly Strategy: first Monday after month close (Codex terra run)
- Adversarial Sunday: 6AM AEST (~40–60 lines, blind spots + rotating deep-dive)

## Pitfalls
- Don't present options without a recommendation.
- Don't reuse last week's content — always fresh evidence.
- If data is missing, mark MISSING and say what would resolve it.
