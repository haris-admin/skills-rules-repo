# Gumby 1000-Point Assessment Framework

**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-05-29 | **Created by:** Pluto (from Gumby/GenSparks methodology)

## Purpose

Score every candidate startup/project idea on a standardized 7-dimension framework totaling 1000 points. This feeds into the GenSparks grading system (A/B/C/KILLED) and provides an objective, comparable scorecard for the decision matrix.

## Framework

| # | Dimension | Max Points | What it measures |
|---|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Market Timing | 200 | Is the market forming, building, or peaking? Urgency of need. Is there a regulatory deadline? |
| 2 | TAM & Market Size | 200 | Addressable market size and growth trajectory. Is this a niche or broad market? |
| 3 | Competitive Moat | 150 | Defensibility. Can a competitor copy this in a weekend? Network effects? Regulatory barriers? |
| 4 | Revenue Velocity | 150 | How fast to first dollar? Is there an impulse-buy tier? Enterprise sales cycle length? |
| 5 | Build Feasibility | 100 | Can this be MVP'd for AUD $250? Can a single developer build it in days? |
| 6 | Portfolio Synergy | 100 | Does this extend existing projects? Same buyer persona? Leverages fleet infrastructure? |
| 7 | Regulatory Tailwind | 100 | Is there a regulatory deadline driving demand? New law creating urgency? Government mandate? |
| | **TOTAL** | **1000** | |

## Scoring Guidelines

### Market Timing (200)
- **180-200:** Immediate urgency — active regulatory deadline, news cycle peak, window closing
- **140-179:** Market building — trend accelerating, early adopters converting, 6-12 month window
- **100-139:** Market forming — early signals, 12-24 month horizon, pioneers needed
- **<100:** Market dormant — no urgency, speculative

### TAM & Market Size (200)
- **160-200:** Mass market — every Australian [founder/SME/fintech], 10K+ potential users
- **120-159:** Vertical market — thousands of buyers, growing sector
- **80-119:** Niche market — hundreds of buyers, bounded growth
- **<80:** Micro-niche — dozens of buyers, limited scale

### Competitive Moat (150)
- **130-150:** Strong moat — regulatory barriers, network effects, exclusive data/partnerships
- **100-129:** Medium moat — first-mover + domain expertise + compliance angle
- **70-99:** Weak moat — replicable but requires trust/relationships
- **<70:** No moat — easily copied, commodity

### Revenue Velocity (150)
- **130-150:** Impulse buy — $39-99 tier, self-serve, instant conversion
- **100-129:** Medium — $149-299/mo, short sales cycle, credit card purchase
- **70-99:** Slow — enterprise sales cycle, procurement required
- **<70:** Very slow — government/enterprise only, 6+ month sales cycle

### Build Feasibility (100)
- **90-100:** Build in 2-3 days, single dev, no external dependencies
- **70-89:** Build in 1 week, needs domain expert review
- **50-69:** Build in 2-4 weeks, needs integration partners
- **<50:** Requires team, funding, or regulatory approval before MVP

### Portfolio Synergy (100)
- **80-100:** Direct extension of existing project, same buyer, leverages fleet infra
- **60-79:** Complements existing project, adjacent buyer persona
- **40-59:** Standalone, some thematic overlap
- **<40:** Completely disconnected, no synergy

### Regulatory Tailwind (100)
- **80-100:** Regulatory deadline with penalties, mandatory compliance, government mandate
- **60-79:** Active regulatory debate, strong tailwind, policy consultation open
- **40-59:** Relevant regulation exists but no deadline pressure
- **<40:** No regulatory push, purely market-driven

## Grading Scale

| Total Score | GenSparks Grade | Verdict |
|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| 800-1000 | **A** | STRONG BUY — ship immediately |
| 700-799 | **A- / B+** | BUY — build in priority order |
| 600-699 | **B** | HOLD — monitor, revisit when timing improves |
| <600 | **C / KILLED** | KILL — opportunity cost too high |

## Output Format

Always produce the scorecard in a decision matrix:

| | Idea 1 | Idea 2 | Idea 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score | 800 | 755 | 745 |
| Grade | A | A- | B+ |
| Market Timing | Forming→Building | Forming | Building |
| Competition | Indirect | None | None |
| Revenue Velocity | Fast ($39) | Medium ($149) | Slow ($99) |
| Portfolio Synergy | Low | High | Medium |
| Build Effort | 3-5 days | 2-3 days | 2-3 days |
| Moat Strength | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Regulatory Push | Medium | Strong | Weak |

Then a clear ranked recommendation with reasoning: "BUILD ORDER: 1. Idea X because..."

## Pitfalls
- Do NOT score based on personal preference — use the dimensional criteria
- Do NOT skip dimensions — every idea gets all 7 scored
- Do NOT inflate scores to make ideas look better — a 745 is a solid B+, not a failure
- The AUD $250 MVP constraint is real — if it can't be built for that, Build Feasibility must be scored down
