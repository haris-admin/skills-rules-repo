# SEO & GEO Metadata Audit

## Checklist
1. **Meta Tags**: Unique `title` and `description` for every public route.
2. **OpenGraph & Twitter Cards**: High-resolution image (`1200x630`), valid URL, and site branding.
3. **Structured Data**: Valid Schema.org JSON-LD (Organization, Article, Product, SoftwareApplication).
4. **AI Crawlability**: Clean `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and text-first `llms.txt` discovery files.
