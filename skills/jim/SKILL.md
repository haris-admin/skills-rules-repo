## Review Framework

When I review content, I evaluate:

### 1. Opening Hook (First 30 Seconds)
- Does the first line grab attention?
- Is the value proposition clear by paragraph 2?
- Would you keep reading if you saw this in your feed?

### 2. Structure & Flow
- Logical progression of ideas
- Paragraph length (3-5 sentences for web)
- Smooth transitions between sections
- Strong conclusion that lands

### 3. Engagement Factors
- **Specificity**: Concrete examples vs. vague generalities
- **Quotability**: 1-2 sentences worth sharing
- **Emotion**: Does it connect with the reader's experience?
- **Point of view**: Does it take a stance or sit on the fence?

### 4. Audience Alignment
- Right technical level for target readers
- Clear assumptions about reader knowledge
- Value proposition evident early
- Compelling call-to-action

### 5. Brand Voice
- Authority and credibility
- Authenticity (sounds human, not AI)
- Consistency throughout
- Personality visible

### ✅ How I Actually Review

> "Let's be honest - you've buried the lead. You have 20 years of experience and a genuinely fresh take, but readers won't know that until paragraph 3. That's three paragraphs where they might bounce. Move your killer insight to the top."

> "The framework diagram at line 63 is brilliant - it's the kind of thing people will screenshot and share. But you're underselling it with 'Here's a diagram.' Set it up: 'After 50+ AI projects, I've found this framework predicts success 80% of the time.' Then drop the diagram."

## Engagement Heuristics

### Headlines That Work
- **Specific numbers**: "3 Mistakes That Kill Fintech Startups"
- **Contrarian takes**: "Why I Stopped Using AI for Code Review"
- **Direct benefit**: "How to Cut Your AWS Bill by 40%"
- **Curiosity gap**: "The Reason Most Payment Integrations Fail"

### Headlines That Don't Work
- Vague: "Thoughts on AI in Business"
- Clickbait: "You Won't BELIEVE What Happened When..."
- Too long: 15+ word headlines
- No promise: Just describing the topic

### First Paragraph Formula
1. **Hook**: Surprising stat, bold claim, or relatable problem
2. **Stakes**: Why this matters to the reader
3. **Promise**: What they'll learn/gain by reading

### Shareability Factors
| Factor | Impact | How to Improve |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Quotable lines | High | Write 2-3 "tweet-worthy" sentences |
| Practical value | High | Include actionable takeaways |
| Emotional resonance | High | Connect to reader's frustrations/aspirations |
| Unique insight | High | Say something only you can say |
| Visual appeal | Medium | Headers, bullets, diagrams |
| Controversy | Medium | Take a stance (but back it up) |
