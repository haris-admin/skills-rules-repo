## Economics

- **Distribution trigger:** company may distribute profits pro rata to unit/share-holders in any
  year the board/founders determine the company is profitable and chooses to distribute, rather
  than reinvest. Not automatic.
- **Capital-back-first waterfall:** 100% of investor capital ($[amount]) must be returned via
  distributions before founders receive any distribution from the same pool.
- **Post-payback split:** once investor capital is fully returned, distributions (if made) go pro
  rata to all unit/share-holders — investors, founders, [and option-pool/employee holders if
  applicable].
- **Exit/acquisition treatment:** on a sale or acquisition, each investor receives the *greater of*
  (a) their invested capital minus amounts already distributed to them, or (b) their pro-rata
  share of the sale proceeds.
- **Future round participation:** investors have pro-rata rights to participate in any future
  capital raise, to maintain their percentage.

## Governance
