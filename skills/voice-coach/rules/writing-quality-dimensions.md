# Writing Quality Dimensions (the positive space)

These score the WRITING against the genre named in the ingested brief, NOT the author against a human/AI boundary. Because they judge the text against a genre standard, they carry no false-positive bias toward ESL or neurodivergent writers. Each dimension is a From/To: where weaker writing of this genre sits, and where stronger writing sits. The coach picks the one or two dimensions with the biggest gap and pairs them with a move in `voice-development.md`.

Never return a number for these when `metric_suppression` is true; describe the From/To position in words instead. These are a developmental picture, never a grade or a gate.

---

## 1. Macrostructure (genre moves)

- **From:** sections present but out of order, or a canonical move missing (e.g. a discussion that re-reports results instead of interpreting them).
- **To:** all canonical moves for the genre present, in order, and proportionate. For a CARS-style introduction: establish the territory, find the gap, occupy it. For empirical work: IMRaD, with the discussion actually interpreting rather than re-reporting.
- **Genre-aware:** map against the genre named in the ingested brief; do not impose IMRaD on an essay or CARS on a lab report.

## 2. Signposting / metadiscourse

- **From:** the reader has to infer the argument's shape; transitions are absent or generic.
- **To:** the reader is told where they are and where they are going, with metadiscourse that matches the discipline's register (not over-signposted, not under-signposted).

## 3. Specificity and evidence

- **From:** general claims ("this is significant") stand without the specific fact or citation that earns them.
- **To:** each claim is anchored to a specific, sourced detail; significance is shown, not asserted. (Direct inverse of ai-tells rules 1 and 2.)

## 4. Argument and stance

- **From:** the passage summarises sources without taking or defending a position.
- **To:** a clear position is stated and defended; counter-positions are acknowledged where the genre expects it.

## 5. Cohesion and sentence-level clarity

- **From:** sentences are individually fine but do not connect; given/new information flow is broken.
- **To:** each sentence picks up the previous one's thread; the paragraph reads as one moving argument.

## 6. Source integration

- **From:** sources are quoted or dropped in without being woven into the student's own reasoning.
- **To:** sources are paraphrased and integrated to serve the student's claim, with citation, in the brief's required style.

---

## Scoring stance (read this before scoring)

- Score the text against the genre, never the author against a class boundary.
- Do not invent a rubric criterion that is not in the ingested brief or rubric. If the brief is silent on a dimension, say the brief is silent and do not score it.
- One or two highest-gap dimensions per pass. The goal is the smallest next step, not a full audit.
