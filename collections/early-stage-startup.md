# Early-Stage Startup Collection

Use this collection to move from a promising observation to a defensible next
decision. It is a discovery map, not a new skill or required sequence: start at
the point that matches the question, then combine only the skills that reduce
the next uncertainty.

## Founder workflow

1. **Frame the opportunity.** Define the customer, painful problem, current alternative, and the decision the work must inform. Start with Zain Dreamer.
2. **Find evidence.** Research market signals, competitors, customers, and assumptions with Deep Research. Use Grounded Citations whenever the result will be shared or used to support a pitch, memo, or decision.
3. **Validate with people.** Use Zain Dreamer to shape customer-discovery questions, willingness-to-pay tests, and the smallest credible pilot; record what was actually said rather than treating interest as commitment.
4. **Make the model work.** Use Habib CFO for pricing and packaging hypotheses, unit economics, cash required to reach the next proof point, and downside scenarios.
5. **Sharpen the narrative.** Use Haris Serial Entrepreneur to challenge the founder case, business-model logic, differentiation, and pitch. Keep claims tied to the evidence gathered above.
6. **Plan the pilot.** State the target customer, offer, success/kill criteria, owner, cost ceiling, and review date. Turn discussion notes into owned actions with Meeting Action Items.
7. **Close the loop.** Track customer, partner, advisor, and investor commitments in Relationship Manager so interviews, pilot conversations, and warm introductions receive timely follow-up.

## Scope boundary

This collection covers pre-build and early-commercial learning: opportunity validation, market sizing, customer discovery, competitive research, pricing, business models, pitch refinement, pilots, and founder/partner follow-through. Use the repository's engineering, deployment, and operations skills only after a specific product or operating need has been established.
