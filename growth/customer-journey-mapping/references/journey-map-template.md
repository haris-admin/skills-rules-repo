# Customer Journey Map Template & Lifecycle Matrix

A customer journey map charts the chronological end-to-end experience of a customer across multiple touchpoints, tracking actions, thoughts, and emotional highs and lows.

---

## 1. The Six Lifecycle Stages

```
┌───────────┐     ┌───────────────┐     ┌──────────┐     ┌────────────┐     ┌───────────┐     ┌──────────┐
│ AWARENESS │ ──► │ CONSIDERATION │ ──► │ DECISION │ ──► │ ONBOARDING │ ──► │ RETENTION │ ──► │ ADVOCACY │
└───────────┘     └───────────────┘     └──────────┘     └────────────┘     └───────────┘     └──────────┘
```

## 2. Customer Journey Matrix Template

| Lifecycle Stage | User Goal / Job | Touchpoints & Channels | User Thoughts & Questions | Emotional State (+/-) | Key Pain Points | Growth Opportunity |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|:---|
| **1. Awareness** | Understand upcoming regulatory compliance requirements | Industry blog, LinkedIn post, peer conversation | *"Will my firm be audited under the new legislation?"* | Neutral (0) | Vague, confusing regulatory legalese on government portals | Publish plain-English compliance summaries and risk checkers |
| **2. Consideration** | Evaluate software solutions to automate compliance | Google Search, product website, feature comparison | *"How much does this cost? Is it compliant with AUSTRAC?"* | Anxious (-1) | Competitors require booking a sales call just to see pricing | Transparent pricing calculator and instant interactive demo |
| **3. Decision** | Choose vendor and gain internal partner sign-off | Pricing page, 1:1 demo call, security documentation | *"Will this integrate with our current legal practice software?"* | Relieved (+1) | Fear of long multi-month implementation contracts | 14-day zero-risk trial with 3 free audit searches included |
| **4. Onboarding** | Run first entity search and save compliance record | Web portal, onboarding checklist, magic link | *"Is this search accurate? How fast can I get the certificate?"* | Skeptical (-1) $\rightarrow$ Delight (+2) | Asking for credit card before showing any value | Deliver "Aha!" moment in $< 60$ seconds with instant preview |
| **5. Retention** | Ongoing monthly due diligence and annual reporting | Weekly email digest, portal dashboard, Slack alerts | *"Is my firm audit-ready for the March 31 deadline?"* | Confident (+2) | Forgetting to check ongoing monitoring updates | Proactive push alerts for changes in business status |
| **6. Advocacy** | Recommend solution to peers in legal/accounting associations | Referral link, industry conferences, Law Society event | *"This saved us 15 hours a week—every partner should use this."* | Proud (+2) | No easy way to share or export branded compliance audit packs | Provide co-branded compliance export badges for clients |
