# Customer Journey Mapping Architecture

A user-centered diagnostic framework to visualize and optimize the end-to-end customer experience across all touchpoints, thoughts, emotions, and friction points.

## When to Use

- **Onboarding & Retention Optimization**: When identifying why users abandon accounts before experiencing core value.
- **Cross-Functional Alignment**: When unifying product, engineering, customer success, and marketing teams around a single customer narrative.
- **Feature Gap Identification**: When uncovering unspoken customer frustrations and emotional low points across their lifecycle.
- **Product Redesign & PLG Strategy**: When eliminating friction valleys, reducing time-to-value (TTV), and building viral referral loops.

---

## The 6 Lifecycle Stages & Analysis Dimensions

```
  Stages:     Awareness ──► Consideration ──► Decision ──► Onboarding ──► Retention ──► Advocacy
             ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Dimensions: • User Goals & Intent
              • Touchpoints & Physical Artifacts
              • Thoughts & Silent Doubts
              • Emotional Curve (+2 Delight to -2 Frustration)
              • Pain Points & Friction Valleys
              • High-Leverage Growth Opportunities
```

### 1. Awareness & Consideration
- Map how prospects encounter your brand, what problems they are seeking to solve, and the friction they face when researching solutions.

### 2. Decision & Onboarding
- Chart the transition from curiosity to commitment. Deliver the **"Aha!" moment** in the first session while tracking the emotional valence curve.

### 3. Retention & Advocacy
- Document the daily/weekly operating rhythm of retained users, identifying features that create habitual stickiness and trigger spontaneous peer referrals.

---

## 4-Step Mapping Procedure

1. **Select Persona & Scenario**: Anchor the journey to a specific [Buyer Persona](../customer-persona-icp/SKILL.md) and explicit objective (e.g. *"First-time compliance check for a high-risk client"*).
2. **Plot Chronological Stages**: Detail the touchpoints and actions the customer experiences across all six stages.
3. **Chart the Emotional Valence Curve**: Assign scores from $-2$ (Frustration) to $+2$ (Delight) at each step to highlight drop-off valleys.
4. **Identify Interventions**: Formulate high-leverage product, copy, or engineering fixes to lift low points into moments of relief.
