# Customer Persona & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Architecture

A systematic methodology for defining, researching, and validating **B2B Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)** and **Buyer Personas** to focus engineering, sales, and marketing resources on high-conversion, low-churn customers.

## When to Use

- **New Product & GTM Strategy**: When defining who the product is built for, what problems it solves, and why they will pay.
- **Lead Qualification & Disqualification**: When establishing objective criteria to filter inbound leads and outbound target accounts.
- **Messaging & Positioning Alignment**: When writing website landing page copy, outbound email campaigns, or ad creative that speaks directly to a specific stakeholder's emotional and functional pain points.
- **Sales Cycle Acceleration**: When mapping multi-threaded buying committees (Champions, Economic Buyers, Compliance Gatekeepers, Blockers).

## The ICP & Persona Discovery Framework

```
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │         1. FIRM-LEVEL IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (ICP)     │
  │   Industry · Regulatory Triggers · Revenue · Team Size │
  └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                              │
                              ▼
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │         2. HUMAN-LEVEL BUYER PERSONA PROFILES          │
  │      Job Title · Authority · Direct Responsibilities   │
  └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                              │
                              ▼
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │         3. JOBS TO BE DONE (JTBD) & PAIN MATRIX        │
  │     Functional Job · Emotional Job · Cost of Inaction  │
  └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
                              │
                              ▼
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │         4. BUYING COMMITTEE DYNAMICS                   │
  │    Champion · Economic Buyer · Gatekeeper · Blocker    │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Define the Company Profile (ICP)
- Specify firmographics: company size, vertical industry, geographic jurisdiction, revenue tier, and technical stack.
- Identify **compelling regulatory triggers** (e.g. AUSTRAC reporting obligations, PCI-DSS compliance, annual audit deadlines).

### 2. Identify the Buying Committee
- **The Champion**: Directly experiences the operational pain and drives internal enthusiasm.
- **The Economic Buyer**: Controls the P&L budget and evaluates return on investment (ROI).
- **The Gatekeeper**: Focuses on risk mitigation, security, and statutory compliance.

### 3. Extract the Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)
- **Functional**: What concrete, measurable task must be completed?
- **Emotional**: How do they want to feel (e.g. audit-proof, stress-free, respected)?
- **Social**: How does this affect how peers, partners, and regulators perceive them?
