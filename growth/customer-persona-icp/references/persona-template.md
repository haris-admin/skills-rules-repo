# Buyer Persona Profile Card Template

Use this structured template to document individual decision-maker personas within your target ICP.

---

## Persona Profile: [Persona Name / Archetype Title]

### 1. Identity & Demographics
- **Job Title**: (e.g. Managing Partner, Compliance Director, General Manager)
- **Reporting Line**: (Who do they report to? e.g. Board of Directors, Managing Director)
- **Experience Level**: (e.g. 15+ years in legal practice / hospitality management)
- **Time in Role**: (e.g. 3–7 years)

### 2. Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)
- **Core Functional Job**: What core task are they hired to accomplish? (e.g. Ensure firm complies with AML/CTF rules without slowing client onboarding).
- **Emotional Job**: How do they want to feel while doing it? (e.g. Confident, in control, audit-proof, stress-free).
- **Social Job**: How do they want to be perceived by peers and clients? (e.g. Reputable, professional, modern, diligent).

### 3. Pain Points & Frustrations
- **Primary Pain**: What keeps them awake at night? (e.g. Fear of AUSTRAC enforcement notices, missed sanctions checks).
- **Operational Friction**: Where do they waste hours each week? (e.g. Manually querying ASIC registers and copy-pasting into Word docs).
- **Cost of Inaction**: What happens if they do nothing? (e.g. Hefty regulatory fines, loss of practicing certificate, reputational ruin).

### 4. Buying Triggers & Compelling Events
- **Internal Triggers**: New partner onboarding, rapid increase in customer transaction volume, legacy software price hike.
- **External Triggers**: AUSTRAC annual compliance reporting deadline (March 31), high-profile regulatory enforcement in their sector, new statutory legislation.

### 5. Objection Landscape
- *"We already have a manual checklist in place."* $\rightarrow$ Show audit risk of manual omission.
- *"We are too busy to implement a new platform right now."* $\rightarrow$ Prove onboarding takes under 15 minutes.
- *"It is too expensive."* $\rightarrow$ Compare monthly subscription against 2 hours of billable partner time saved.
