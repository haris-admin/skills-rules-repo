# Portfolio Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

Concrete, ready-to-use ICP and persona definitions for portfolio ventures.

---

## 1. AML Hive: The Mid-Tier Legal & Accounting Managing Partner

### Target ICP (Firm Level)
- **Firmographics**: Australian boutique and mid-tier law firms, conveyancing practices, and accounting partnerships (5–40 fee earners).
- **Regulatory Status**: AUSTRAC Reporting Entities (or Tranche 2 expansion entities preparing for oversight).
- **Technology Profile**: Smokeball, Leap, Actionstep, or Xero Practice Manager.
- **Pain Threshold**: Executes 20–100 customer onboardings/month with manual PDF identity checks and no centralized audit trail.

### Key Persona: "Prudent Peter" (Managing Partner / Compliance Officer)
- **Daily Struggle**: Worries that junior solicitors or clerks are skipping PEP/sanctions verification steps to close deals faster.
- **Buying Trigger**: Upcoming AUSTRAC compliance review deadline, partner retreat reviewing professional indemnity risks, or new anti-money laundering legislation.
- **Winning Pitch**: *"Turn a 45-minute manual ASIC and PEP search process into a 60-second, audit-proof compliance certificate with zero IT setup."*

## 2. Tapease: The High-Volume Hospitality Venue Owner

### Target ICP (Merchant Level)
- **Firmographics**: Multi-venue cafes, bustling restaurants, bars, and boutique retail (A$50,000 – A$300,000 monthly card turnover).
- **Infrastructure**: Using legacy countertop EFTPOS terminals or looking to integrate with modern POS (Clover, Square, Lightspeed).
- **Pain Threshold**: Frustrated by opaque merchant fee statements, hidden interchange markups, and delayed settlement batches.

### Key Persona: "Efficient Elena" (Hospitality Venue Operator)
- **Daily Struggle**: Razor-thin hospitality margins eaten up by hidden 2.5%+ card processing fees and clunky end-of-day reconciliation.
- **Buying Trigger**: Opening a second venue, renegotiating terminal leases, or hitting end-of-financial-year fee reviews.
- **Winning Pitch**: *"Next-day settlement, transparent flat rates, and zero terminal lock-in contracts."*

## 3. Simplifii-OS: The Autonomous AI Lead Developer

### Target ICP (Company Level)
- **Firmographics**: Fast-moving tech startups, boutique development agencies, and AI engineering teams (3–25 developers).
- **Stack**: Node/TypeScript, React/Next.js, Python FastAPI, using Claude Code, Codex, or Cursor.
- **Pain Threshold**: Spending 40%+ of developer hours on boilerplate, context maintenance across multiple AI agent tools, and manual testing.

### Key Persona: "Autonomous Alex" (Founding Engineer / Tech Lead)
- **Daily Struggle**: Agent context drift, inconsistent tool execution, and spending nights fixing repetitive integration bugs.
- **Buying Trigger**: Launching a major v2 product overhaul, expanding team velocity without expanding headcount.
- **Winning Pitch**: *"Unified skill execution, cross-tool context portability, and automated verification loops that stop hallucinations before git commit."*
