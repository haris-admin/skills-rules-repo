# B2B Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Framework

An Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) defines the specific type of company or organization that receives the highest value from your product, has the shortest sales cycle, the highest retention, and the lowest cost to serve.

---

## 1. ICP vs Buyer Persona

- **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**: Focuses on the **company/firm** (firmographics, industry, regulatory status, revenue, tech stack).
- **Buyer Persona**: Focuses on the **human decision-maker** within that company (title, job responsibilities, daily pain points, personal KPIs, career fears).

---

## 2. Core ICP Dimension Matrix

| Dimension | Key Attributes to Define | High-Value Signal | Disqualification Red Flag |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Industry & Verticals** | Specific industry niche (e.g. legal, accounting, real estate, remittance, merchant POS). | Operating in a strictly regulated environment with mandatory compliance obligations. | Unregulated hobbyist market with zero compliance liability. |
| **Company Size** | Employee count, partner count, transaction volume. | 5–50 employees (fast decision cycle; sufficient budget). | Solopreneur with no budget OR 5,000+ enterprise requiring 18-month procurement. |
| **Regulatory Triggers** | AUSTRAC reporting entity status, ASIC registration, PCI-DSS compliance. | Active reporting entity facing external audit deadlines or regulatory reform enforcement. | Zero regulatory oversight or statutory obligations. |
| **Technology Stack** | Current practice management, accounting, or billing tools. | Uses modern web software (Smokeball, Clio, Xero, Stripe, Clover). | Legacy on-premise mainframe with locked-down airgapped network. |
| **Financial Profile** | Annual revenue, average customer contract value. | A$1M – A$20M revenue; transaction fee threshold > A$50k/mo. | Pre-revenue or near-insolvent cash position. |

---

## 3. The Four Stakeholder Roles in B2B Deals

1. **The Champion**: The internal user who feels the daily pain, loves your product, and actively sells it internally to leadership.
2. **The Economic Buyer**: The person with P&L authority and final budget sign-off (Managing Partner, CFO, Owner).
3. **The Gatekeeper / Compliance Officer**: The risk-averse reviewer who must verify legal, security, and privacy compliance (Legal Counsel, MLRO).
4. **The Blocker**: The stakeholder whose existing workflow or status is threatened by the change (e.g. incumbent vendor, entrenched ops manager).
