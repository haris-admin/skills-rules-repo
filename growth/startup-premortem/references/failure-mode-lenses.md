# Failure-Mode Lenses

Ask each question of the specific plan. If a lens finds nothing, say so; do not pad.

| Lens | Questions |
|---|---|
| Evidence | How many primary conversations, replies, LOIs, live integrations exist? Which "verified" claims rest on one secondary source? Do documents cite each other in a circle? |
| Thesis drift | Do all documents describe the same product, buyer, and price? Does the validation scorecard test the current venture? |
| Buyer | Who signs? What is their vendor-risk process (insurance, security attestation, references)? Can they build, buy a bundle, or white-label an incumbent instead? What is the sales cycle versus the runway? |
| Price vs value | Price as a share of the customer's total spend on the thing managed. Measured saving per unit versus price. Who actually pays (employer, employee, host)? |
| Market arithmetic | Recompute SOM from units x price x usage. Does the target unit count reach the stated GTV or revenue? Do TAM inputs reproduce the TAM? |
| Supply side / cold start | Which counterparties must join first? What do they get, and what does joining cost them (legal, KYC, tariffs, engineering)? Who refuses (big players) and does that break the headline promise? |
| Product scope | List everything needed for the promise to be true (apps, SDK, cards, integrations, settlement, support). Compare with team size and hours. Is the "prototype" mock data? |
| Hardware / platform dependency | Does the promise rely on a device, OS, or partner behaviour never tested? Name the cheapest physical test. |
| Money flow | Float, credit risk, payout cost per transaction, working capital. Principal versus agent for tax invoices. Licensing (payment facility, AML/CTF, consumer law). |
| Policy / timing | Is the demand wedge a subsidy, exemption, or rule? Its legislated state and dates. |
| Incumbent response | Can/won't test. Who can add this feature in a quarter once demand is proven? Who is already adjacent? |
| Founder / team | Hours per week, other ventures, full-time statements across documents, claim wording versus repo reality, key-person risk. |
| Funding path | Sequencing and dependencies between funding sources. Cash timing (refunds arrive after year end). Milestones required by grants versus milestones achieved. |
| Process | Are agents producing documents faster than the founder holds conversations? Are rules (stage gates) honoured by the artefacts in the repo? |
