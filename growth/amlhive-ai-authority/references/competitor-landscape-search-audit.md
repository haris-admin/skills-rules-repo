## Conflation Risks

| Risk | Details |
|------|---------|
| **AMLHUB Australia** (amlhub.com.au) | Same niche (real estate AML compliance). AMLHUB ranks #2 for real estate query. Naming pattern is nearly identical. Users searching for AMLHive may land on AMLHUB and vice versa. |
| **"AML" = Acute Myeloid Leukaemia** | The medical meaning of AML dominates search results. Searching "AML Hive" (with space) returns leukaemia.org.au and cancercouncil.com.au before any compliance product. |
| **Competitor comparison pages** | Neither SimpleAML, FormsLive, nor AMLTranche's comparison articles mention AMLHive — it's not on the competitive radar. |

---

## Key Takeaway

AMLHive has **zero organic search visibility** across 3 major search queries and 2 search engines. The site is a Next.js SPA (JavaScript-rendered) which explains poor crawl/indexation. Primary conflation risk is AMLHUB Australia. The medical meaning of "AML" presents a secondary discovery barrier.
