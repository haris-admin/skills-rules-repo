AMLHive is Australia's purpose-built AML/CTF compliance platform for independent real estate agencies preparing for AUSTRAC Tranche 2 (effective 1 July 2026).

Designed for principals and small compliance teams who need to meet their obligations without hiring a full-time compliance consultant, AMLHive brings every required workflow into one guided platform:

**Client Screening** — Automated sanctions and politically exposed person (PEP) screening on every client, matched against international watchlists. Results are recorded with a full audit trail.

**KYC / KYB Verification** — Identity verification (KYC) for individuals and entity verification (KYB) for companies, trusts, and other business structures. The system routes each entity type to the correct CDD tier automatically.

**Customer Due Diligence (CDD)** — Guided CDD checklists with automated risk tier assignment (Simplified, Standard, or Enhanced). Risk factors are recorded, reviewed, and locked in the audit trail.

**10-Step AML/CTF Program Builder** — A complete wizard that guides agencies through building their AML/CTF program — from business profile and designated services through to governance, risk assessment, and principal sign-off. Produces a PDF-ready program document.

**Report Templates (SMR / TTR / CBM)** — Suspicious Matter Report and Threshold Transaction Report drafting templates with deadline guidance. Drafts are prepared for manual lodgement through AUSTRAC Online.

**Compliance Calendar** — Tracks AUSTRAC deadlines, CDD review dates, program review schedules, and training renewals. Sends alerts before deadlines are missed.

**Staff Training and Certification** — Built-in AML/CTF training modules with completion tracking and digital certificates for every team member.

**Immutable 7-Year Audit Trail** — Every compliance action is recorded in an insert-only audit trail retained for the 7 years AUSTRAC requires.

**Built for Australian independent agencies** — AMLHive is not an adapted overseas product. It is designed specifically for Australian real estate agencies: Australian entity types, Australian screening obligations, AUSTRAC-specific program requirements, and flat monthly pricing with no per-check fees.
