# Pretotyping Experiment Catalog

Pretotyping (Alberto Savoia) tests the appeal and core usage of a potential product by simulating its core experience with the smallest investment of time and money ("Make sure you are building the *right it* before you build *it right*").

---

## The 8 Pretotype Archetypes

### 1. Fake Door / Smoke Test
- **Mechanism**: A landing page with a clear value proposition, pricing table, and a "Start Free Trial" or "Buy Now" button. When clicked, a polite message states: *"We are currently onboarding early-access customers in batches. Leave your email to claim priority access."*
- **Metric**: Button click rate (>8% of qualified visitors indicates strong demand).
- **Cost/Time**: 1 day, $50 ad budget.

### 2. Concierge MVP
- **Mechanism**: Provide the service manually behind the scenes while presenting an automated illusion to the customer. For example, manual ASIC compliance checks performed by the founder before writing search APIs.
- **Metric**: Customer satisfaction, perceived value, and manual time investment per unit.
- **Cost/Time**: 2-3 days setup; low tech overhead.

### 3. Wizard of Oz
- **Mechanism**: A real front-end interface, but backend workflows are executed by humans rather than automated algorithms (e.g., Zappos founder taking shoe photos in physical stores).
- **Metric**: Usage frequency, drop-off rates, and user workflow patterns.
- **Cost/Time**: 1 week frontend prototype.

### 4. YouTube / Video Explainer (The Dropbox Test)
- **Mechanism**: A 2-minute video demonstrating how the software will function using Figma or screen mockups, ending with a waitlist call-to-action.
- **Metric**: Waitlist conversion rate (>15% indicates virality/urgency).
- **Cost/Time**: 2 days.

### 5. Infiltrator Pretotype
- **Mechanism**: Placing an offer or flyer in an existing channel or store where the target audience congregates (e.g., posting a flyer in an accounting hub or business incubator).
- **Metric**: Direct inquiry rate per impression.
- **Cost/Time**: Half day.

### 6. Relabel / Repackage
- **Mechanism**: Use off-the-shelf software (Airtable + Zapier + Softr) repackaged under your brand to test customer workflows before building custom React/FastAPI codebases.
- **Metric**: Daily active usage and retention over 14 days.
- **Cost/Time**: 1-2 days.

### 7. The Deposit Test
- **Mechanism**: Ask customers to commit a refundable $10 to $100 deposit to secure early-bird pricing or priority onboarding.
- **Metric**: Deposit conversion rate (>2% of interested leads).
- **Cost/Time**: Stripe payment link.

### 8. The Letter of Intent (LOI)
- **Mechanism**: For B2B enterprise or mid-market, ask prospective buyers to sign a standard non-binding LOI stating they will pilot the software upon delivery of agreed milestones.
- **Metric**: Signed LOIs (3+ signed LOIs justify building the MVP).
- **Cost/Time**: 1-page legal template.
