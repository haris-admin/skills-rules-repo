# AARRR Pirate Metrics Funnel Breakdown

Formulated by Dave McClure (500 Startups), the AARRR framework organizes startup growth into five distinct customer lifecycle stages: **Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Revenue, and Referral**.

---

## The Five Funnel Stages

```
                ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
                │          ACQUISITION            │
                │     How do customers find you?  │
                └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
                ┌────────────────▼────────────────┐
                │           ACTIVATION            │
                │  Do they have a great 1st exp?  │
                └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
                ┌────────────────▼────────────────┐
                │           RETENTION             │
                │      Do they come back?         │
                └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
                ┌────────────────▼────────────────┐
                │            REVENUE              │
                │    How do you monetize them?    │
                └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                 │
                ┌────────────────▼────────────────┐
                │           REFERRAL              │
                │     Do they tell others?        │
                └─────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Acquisition (Top of Funnel)
**Question**: Where are users coming from, and how many are landing on your product surfaces?

- **Core Metrics**:
  - Unique Visitors / Traffic by Source (Organic Search, Direct, Paid Search, Social, Content, Outbound).
  - Cost Per Click (CPC) and Click-Through Rate (CTR).
  - Top-of-funnel conversion rate: $\frac{\text{Landing Page Visits}}{\text{Impressions}}$.
- **Key Levers**:
  - SEO / Content Authority (zero-click content, programmatic pages, technical guides).
  - High-intent search campaigns (e.g. regulatory keywords).
  - Engineering as marketing (free lookup tools, checkers, calculators).

---

## 2. Activation (The "Aha!" Moment)
**Question**: Do new signups experience the core value proposition on their very first session?

- **Core Metrics**:
  - Visitor-to-Signup Conversion Rate ($\frac{\text{Signups}}{\text{Unique Visitors}}$).
  - Time to Value (TTV): Minutes/hours from account creation to first successful core action.
  - Onboarding Completion Rate (e.g., ran first compliance search, created first project).
- **Key Levers**:
  - Frictionless onboarding (social auth, single-step signup, deferred billing).
  - Pre-populated templates or demo data.
  - Immediate instant feedback on the first action.

---

## 3. Retention (The Growth Foundation)
**Question**: Do activated users return consistently over days, weeks, and months?

- **Core Metrics**:
  - Day 1 / Day 7 / Day 30 Retention.
  - Usage Frequency: Daily Active Users (DAU) / Monthly Active Users (MAU) stickiness ratio.
  - Cohort Churn Rate (Logo Churn & Revenue Churn).
- **Key Levers**:
  - Automated trigger emails & notifications (deadlines, alerts, weekly recaps).
  - Workflow embedding (making the product part of the daily operating rhythm).
  - Team collaboration / multi-user invites.

---

## 4. Revenue (Monetization & Expansion)
**Question**: How effectively does product usage convert into recurring cash flow?

- **Core Metrics**:
  - Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate ($\frac{\text{Paying Customers}}{\text{Total Signups}}$).
  - Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) & Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR).
  - Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) & Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).
  - Net Revenue Retention (NRR).
- **Key Levers**:
  - Value-aligned pricing tiers (charging per search, per seat, or per transaction).
  - Usage-based overages and add-on packs.
  - Annual upfront discounting to lock in cash and reduce churn.

---

## 5. Referral (Viral Engine & Amplification)
**Question**: Do satisfied customers recommend the product to their peers?

- **Core Metrics**:
  - Virality Coefficient ($K$-Factor): $K = i \times c$, where $i$ is invitations sent per user and $c$ is conversion rate per invite ($K > 1$ is viral).
  - Net Promoter Score (NPS) and Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).
  - Referral-driven signups as a percentage of total acquisition.
- **Key Levers**:
  - Two-sided incentive programs (both inviter and invitee receive credits).
  - Built-in collaborative loops ("Powered by" badges on generated reports/invoices).
  - High-touch advocacy and partner channels (associations, accountants, consultants).
