# Market Sizing Pitch & Board Deck Templates

How to present TAM, SAM, and SOM in investor pitch decks, investment memos, and board reviews without triggering skepticism.

---

## 1. The Single Slide Format

### Visual Layout
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               TOTAL ADDRESSABLE MARKET (TAM)                │
│             $30.6M AUD (17,000 AUSTRAC Entities)             │
│  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │          SERVICEABLE ADDRESSABLE MARKET (SAM)         │  │
│  │       $12.2M AUD (6,800 Tranche 2 Accountants & R/E)   │  │
│  │  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │  │
│  │  │        SERVICEABLE OBTAINABLE MARKET (SOM)      │  │  │
│  │  │       $979K ARR by Year 3 (544 Entities / 8%)   │  │  │
│  │  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │  │
│  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The 3 Talking Points
1. **The TAM Anchor**: *"We define TAM strictly from bottom-up registered entities (e.g. 17,000 reporting entities under AUSTRAC mandate) multiplied by our base tier ACV of $1,800/year, representing an immediate $30.6M domestic compliance software market."*
2. **The SAM Focus**: *"Our SAM filters for Tranche 2 professional service providers with 5–50 staff who lack dedicated in-house compliance counsel—6,800 entities representing $12.2M."*
3. **The SOM Execution Path**: *"Our SOM targets 544 firms over 36 months ($979K ARR), driven by our certified accounting integration partner network and SEO compliance authority engine."*

## 2. Common Pitch Deck Red Flags to Avoid

- ❌ **The "1% of China / 1% of Global Market" Fallacy**: Never say *"The global FinTech market is $300B; if we just get 0.1%, we are a $300M company."*
- ❌ **Unsubstantiated ACV**: Never state an ACV of $10,000 without showing comparable software line items already paid by the target buyer.
- ❌ **Static Market Assumption**: Always note whether regulatory deadlines or technological shifts are expanding the addressable pool.
