# context.md (shared agent state)

Purpose: live, short, current. Any agent reads this after AGENTS.md and updates it before ending a session. History goes in git; long-form status goes in docs/current_progress.md.

## Venture in one paragraph
## Current phase and gate
## What we know (evidence, with tags)
## What we do not know (open assumptions, ranked)
## Open decisions for the founder (question, options, recommendation, status)
## Active work (who/which agent, scope, status)
## Do-not-do list (authorisation boundaries: no push, deploy, send, delete without explicit ask)
## Handoff notes (last session, next three actions)
## Change log (date, agent, one line)
