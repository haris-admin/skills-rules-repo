# The Superhuman Product-Market Fit Engine

Rahul Vohra's product-market fit engine reverse-engineered Sean Ellis's 40% benchmark into an actionable, repeatable product optimization cycle.

---

## 1. The 4-Question Survey

Send this survey to users who have experienced the core product at least twice in the last 14 days:

1. **How would you feel if you could no longer use [Product]?**
   - *Very disappointed* (Your High-Expectation Customers - HXC)
   - *Somewhat disappointed*
   - *Not disappointed (it isn't really that useful)*
2. **What type of people do you think would most benefit from [Product]?**
   - (Helps identify the precise persona in customer language)
3. **What is the main benefit you receive from [Product]?**
   - (Reveals the core emotional and operational hook)
4. **How can we improve [Product] for you?**
   - (Surfaces the immediate blockers)

---

## 2. The 40% Rule

- Across hundreds of startups benchmarked by Sean Ellis, companies that achieved sustainable hypergrowth consistently scored **$\ge 40\%$ "Very Disappointed"**.
- Companies scoring $<40\%$ almost always struggle to retain users and burn marketing dollars acquiring customers into a leaky bucket.

---

## 3. The 50/50 Product Roadmap Allocation

Once the survey is scored, split your engineering roadmap 50/50:
1. **50% of roadmap capacity**: Double down on what the "Very Disappointed" users already love. Deepen the moat, polish core UX, and make the best features 10x faster.
2. **50% of roadmap capacity**: Address what holds back the "Somewhat Disappointed" users for whom the main benefit resonated. Ignore users who said "Not Disappointed" entirely (building for them will dilute the core product).
