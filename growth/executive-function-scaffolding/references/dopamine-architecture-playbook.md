# Dopamine Architecture & Executive Function Scaffolding Playbook

Behavioral design principles for ADHD, Autism, and Executive Dysfunction. How to design systems that reduce activation friction, maintain engagement, and avoid shame-based burnout.

---

## 1. The Activation Energy Trap & Micro-Steps

In ADHD brains, dopamine regulation makes initiating a task (the "Activation Energy") drastically harder than continuing a task once engaged.
- **The Anti-Overwhelm Rule**: Never assign a project titled "Write 3,000-Word Thesis". Assign "Step 1: Open a blank doc and paste the title (3 minutes)".
- **The Pareto 5-Step Model**: Every major assignment, proposal, or software sprint is decomposed into exactly 5 sequential micro-steps. Completing Step 1 creates a dopamine hit that lowers the threshold for Step 2.

---

## 2. Dopamine Scaffolding Mechanics

### A. Immediate Micro-Feedback
- When a step is checked off, deliver immediate, rewarding visual feedback:
  - Checkbox morphs into a soft glowing checkmark (`#34d399`).
  - Subtle progress bar fills by exactly 20%.
  - Micro-celebration copy: *"Activation hurdle cleared! Step 1 of 5 locked."*

### B. Streak-Repair (No Shame Mechanics)
- Traditional gamified apps (Duolingo, habit trackers) use punishing streak mechanics: miss one day, and your 30-day streak drops to zero. For ADHD users, this triggers the **"All-or-Nothing Abandonment Effect"**, causing them to delete the app in shame.
- **The Repair Rule**: Provide automatic "Streak Freezes" and gentle return messages: *"Welcome back! Your streak was preserved while you recharged."*

---

## 3. Time Blindness Calibration

ADHD brains perceive time in two zones: **"Now"** and **"Not Now"**.
- **Visual Timers**: Use circular pie-chart or elapsed-progress bars rather than ticking numerical seconds (ticking digits increase heart rate and anxiety).
- **Estimated vs Actual Calibration**: Prompt users to guess how long Step 1 will take (e.g. 15 min). When it takes 8 minutes, mirror that back: *"You finished in 8 minutes—7 minutes faster than expected!"* This systematically recalibrates temporal confidence.
