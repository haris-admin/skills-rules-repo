# Startup Capability Matrix & Competency Mapping

A structured audit template to evaluate a startup's current team competencies against the requirements of its next milestone (e.g., MVP launch, Seed fundraising, AUSTRAC compliance audit, scaling B2B sales).

---

## 1. The Core Competency Matrix

Score each domain on a 1–5 maturity scale:
- **1 - Absent**: Zero capability; major single point of failure or critical blind spot.
- **2 - Basic**: Ad-hoc knowledge; can perform tasks slowly with frequent guidance or errors.
- **3 - Competent**: Independent execution; satisfies ordinary day-to-day requirements.
- **4 - Advanced**: High velocity; deep domain mastery; capable of mentoring others.
- **5 - World-Class**: Industry-leading capability; sets state-of-the-art standards.

## 2. Functional Domain Audit

| **7. People & Operations** | Role scorecards, structured hiring, contractor coordination, 30-60-90 onboarding | 3 | 1 | **+2 (Critical)** | Ops Lead |

## 3. Vulnerability & Dependency Scan

1. **Key-Person Risk (Bus Factor)**: If a single engineer or founder is unavailable for 3 weeks, which critical systems halt entirely?
2. **Regulatory Risk Exposure**: Are statutory compliance obligations (e.g. AUSTRAC annual report, tax filings, PCI audits) owned by qualified domain specialists?
3. **Execution Velocity Bottleneck**: Which functional gap is currently delaying the delivery of core customer value?
