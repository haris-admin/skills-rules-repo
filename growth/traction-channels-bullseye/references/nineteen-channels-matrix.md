# The 19 Traction Channels Matrix

From Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares' *Traction*, every startup can acquire customers through one of 19 distinct channels. Most founders bias toward the 2–3 channels they know best; the Bullseye framework forces equal consideration of all 19.

---

## Channel Catalog & Startup Applicability

| # | Channel | Description | Cost / Velocity | Primary Metrics | B2B SaaS Playbook |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **1** | **Targeting Blogs** | Engaging niche industry bloggers and niche publication writers. | Low cost / High speed | Referral traffic, backlinks, domain rating | Sponsor niche compliance / fintech blogs; write guest posts for legal/accounting newsletters. |
| **2** | **Publicity (PR)** | Coverage in mainstream and trade media (AFR, TechCrunch, StartupDaily). | Med cost / Low speed | Press mentions, inbound inquiries | Pitch regulatory reform angles (e.g. AUSTRAC Tranche 2 expansion); publish industry benchmark reports. |
| **3** | **Unconventional PR** | Stunts, viral gifts, high-impact public experiments. | Low-Med cost / High speed | Social virality, brand recall | Send physical "mock regulatory audit packs" or "emergency survival kits" to target managing partners. |
| **4** | **Search Engine Marketing (SEM)** | Paid Google Search & Bing ads targeting high-intent buyer queries. | High cost / Immediate | CPC, CTR, Cost per Lead (CPL) | Target high-intent queries: "AML compliance software Australia", "ASIC register lookup API". |
| **5** | **Social & Display Ads** | Sponsored posts on LinkedIn, Meta, YouTube, X, Reddit. | Med-High cost / High speed | Impressions, CTR, Lead form fills | Target LinkedIn job titles: "Chief Compliance Officer", "Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)". |
| **6** | **Offline Ads** | Billboards, radio, industry magazines, transit ads. | High cost / Slow | Brand awareness, promo code redemptions | Place ads in Law Society and CPA Australia print journals. |
| **7** | **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** | Ranking organically for informational and transactional search intent. | Low cash / Slow compounding | Organic impressions, keyword rank, domain authority | Build programmatic pages for every Australian business name and ASIC registration lookup. |
| **8** | **Content Marketing** | Educational blog posts, compliance guides, whitepapers, benchmarks. | Low cash / Medium speed | Pageviews, newsletter signups, time-on-page | Publish step-by-step AML/CTF program guides, audit prep checklists, and regulatory deadline summaries. |
| **9** | **Email Marketing** | Cold outbound, automated drip sequences, newsletters. | Low cost / High speed | Open rate, reply rate, meeting booking rate | Segment cold outreach by regulatory deadline; send weekly industry regulatory digests. |
| **10** | **Viral Marketing** | Built-in product invitation loops and referral incentives. | Low cost / Exponential | Viral coefficient ($K$-factor), invite rate | Include "Audited by AMLHive" badge on exported compliance PDF reports; give credits for referring peer firms. |
| **11** | **Engineering as Marketing** | Free standalone tools, calculators, diagnostic widgets. | Dev time / High longevity | Inbound organic links, tool users $\rightarrow$ trial signups | Build a free "AML/CTF Risk Assessment Calculator" or free ASIC business name verification widget. |
| **12** | **Business Development (BD)** | Strategic partnerships, marketplace integrations, co-selling. | Low cash / Slow-Med | Partner-referred deals, integration installs | Partner with practice management software (Clio, Smokeball, Actionstep) or merchant POS platforms. |
| **13** | **Direct Sales** | Prospecting, cold calling, enterprise demos, consultative closing. | High headcount / Med speed | Pipeline value, win rate, average contract value (ACV) | Outbound consultative outreach to mid-tier accounting firms, real estate agencies, and remittance operators. |
| **14** | **Affiliate Programs** | Revenue share with third parties who refer paying customers. | Performance-only / Med speed | Affiliate conversions, payout per acquisition | Offer 20% recurring commission to compliance consultants and legal advisors who recommend the platform. |
| **15** | **Existing Platforms** | App stores and ecosystems (Clover App Market, Shopify, Salesforce AppExchange). | Low cost / Med speed | App store rank, marketplace installs | Publish Clover/Square merchant apps for Tapease; integrate into workflow marketplaces. |
| **16** | **Trade Shows & Conferences** | Sponsoring, exhibiting, or attending industry expos. | High cost / High touch | Qualified badge scans, booked demos | Exhibit at legal tech summits, accounting expos, and financial crime conferences. |
| **17** | **Offline Events** | Hosting private roundtables, workshops, dinners, breakfast briefings. | Med cost / High conversion | Attendee-to-opportunity rate | Host private breakfast briefings for 15 managing partners on upcoming AUSTRAC regulatory reforms. |
| **18** | **Speaking Engagements** | Keynotes, panel discussions, educational webinars. | Time investment / High authority | Inbound inbound requests, social authority | Present on anti-money laundering compliance panels at industry association webinars. |
| **19** | **Community Building** | Managing user forums, Slack groups, Discord, open-source repos. | Long-term effort / High loyalty | Active community members, user-to-user support | Host an exclusive Slack community for Australian compliance officers and legal practice managers. |
