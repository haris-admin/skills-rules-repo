# SPIN Discovery Call Planner & Agenda

A pre-call discovery planning worksheet designed to map out Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff questions before speaking with a high-value B2B prospect.

---

## 1. Pre-Call Discovery Planner

- **Prospect Company**: (e.g. Acme Legal Partners Pty Ltd)
- **Primary Contact**: (Name, Title, Role in Buying Committee)
- **Known Context (Pre-Call Research)**:
  - Firm size, estimated fee earners, practice focus.
  - Current tech stack (found via job posts or website).
  - Regulatory classification (AUSTRAC reporting entity, state law society).

---

## 2. Structured Question Roadmap

| Stage | Planned Questions for this Account | Expected Prospect Answer / Evidence |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **S — Situation** | *How does your team currently perform company and identity checks during client onboarding?* | Uses manual ASIC web lookups and physical paper photo IDs. |
| **P — Problem** | *Where do delays or manual back-and-forth communication occur most often with clients?* | Waiting days for clients to email back certified copies; junior clerks forget to check PEP lists. |
| **I — Implication** | *When onboarding stalls for 4–5 days, what does that cost you in lost client deals or billable delay? What is your liability if an unverified entity passes through?* | Realizes 5–10% of high-value matters complain about onboarding delay; recognizes personal liability under regulatory rules. |
| **N — Need-Payoff** | *If your team could generate an automated, instant AUSTRAC audit-ready certificate in 60 seconds with zero manual data entry, how would that transform your onboarding workflow?* | Articulates the value: *"It would save us 15 hours a week and eliminate compliance stress entirely."* |

---

## 3. Call Execution Agenda (30-Minute Discovery Flow)

- **00:00 – 03:00 (Rapport & Agenda Alignment)**: Set expectations, state the purpose of understanding their current workflow.
- **03:00 – 08:00 (Situation Questions)**: Confirm baseline operations and current systems without exhausting them.
- **08:00 – 16:00 (Problem & Implication Questions)**: Deeply explore friction points, amplify downstream consequences, and quantify financial/regulatory risk.
- **16:00 – 22:00 (Need-Payoff Questions)**: Guide the prospect to describe the ideal solution and business impact.
- **22:00 – 27:00 (Surgical Solution Demonstration)**: Show only the 1–2 specific features that address their stated need-payoff requirements.
- **27:00 – 30:00 (Securing Next Action)**: Agree on an explicit, scheduled next milestone (pilot, contract review, or team demo).
