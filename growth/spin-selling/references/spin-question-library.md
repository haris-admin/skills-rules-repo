# The SPIN Selling Question Library

Formulated by Neil Rackham after analyzing 35,000 sales interactions, SPIN Selling is a consultative framework that guides prospects through four sequential question stages: **Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff**.

---

## 1. S — Situation Questions (Context & Facts)

**Goal**: Establish baseline operational context without interrogating or boring the prospect with information you could have researched beforehand.

### General B2B Tech Questions:
- *"Can you walk me through how your team currently handles [specific workflow]?"*
- *"What systems or tools are you currently using to manage [process]?"*
- *"How many team members are involved in executing this task on a weekly basis?"*
- *"How long has your current process been in place?"*

### Compliance / AMLHive Domain Questions:
- *"How does your firm currently conduct customer due diligence and identity checks during new client onboarding?"*
- *"What databases or registries do you search when verifying an Australian company or business name?"*
- *"Who within your practice is responsible for logging and archiving AUSTRAC audit trails?"*

---

## 2. P — Problem Questions (Uncovering Friction)

**Goal**: Uncover latent dissatisfaction, bottlenecks, errors, and inefficiencies that the prospect experiences with their current setup.

### General B2B Tech Questions:
- *"Where do delays most frequently occur in your current pipeline?"*
- *"How often do manual errors or omissions slip through your existing review steps?"*
- *"What is the most frustrating part of using your existing tools for your staff?"*
- *"Is your current setup scaling effectively as your volume increases, or is it hitting a ceiling?"*

### Compliance / AMLHive Domain Questions:
- *"How much time do fee earners or clerks spend manually copy-pasting ASIC registry information into Word documents?"*
- *"Have you ever had a client onboarding stalled because a search or verification took days to return?"*
- *"How confident are you that every junior solicitor in the firm strictly checks international PEP and sanctions lists for every high-risk matter?"*

---

## 3. I — Implication Questions (Amplifying the Cost of Inaction)

**Goal**: Transform small, manageable problems into urgent, critical business issues by exploring the downstream consequences, financial costs, and risks.

### General B2B Tech Questions:
- *"When that bottleneck occurs, what effect does it have on your customer delivery deadlines?"*
- *"If that error rate continues at your current growth rate, what will that cost the business annually?"*
- *"How does the friction in this workflow impact team morale and developer/staff turnover?"*
- *"If your competitors solve this while your process remains manual, how does that affect your win rate?"*

### Compliance / AMLHive Domain Questions:
- *"If an AUSTRAC audit was initiated tomorrow and discovered missing PEP records on client files from 6 months ago, what statutory penalties or public enforcement notices would the partnership face?"*
- *"How does spending 3 to 4 hours per partner per week on manual admin checks affect your billable revenue targets?"*
- *"If a client onboarding takes 5 days because of manual verification delays, how many prospective clients walk away to a faster competitor?"*

---

## 4. N — Need-Payoff Questions (Guiding Value Articulation)

**Goal**: Get the prospect to state the benefits, value, and emotional relief of solving the problem in their own words.

### General B2B Tech Questions:
- *"If we could reduce that turnaround time from days to under 5 minutes, how would that impact your quarterly delivery targets?"*
- *"What would it mean for your team if all repetitive data collection was automated and verified in real time?"*
- *"How would having an instant executive dashboard help you report progress to the board?"*

### Compliance / AMLHive Domain Questions:
- *"If your staff could generate an audit-proof, comprehensive AML/CTF compliance certificate in 60 seconds with one click, how much partner billable time would that reclaim each month?"*
- *"How would having a centralized, permanent audit vault give you peace of mind when AUSTRAC compliance reporting season arrives?"*
- *"If client onboarding became instantaneous, how would that improve your firm's reputation for modern, premium client service?"*
