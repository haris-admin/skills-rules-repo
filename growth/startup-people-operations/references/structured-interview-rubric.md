# Structured Interview & Candidate Evaluation Rubric

Unstructured "coffee chats" produce biased, inaccurate hiring decisions. A structured interview process evaluates candidates against the explicit outcomes and competencies defined in the Role Scorecard.

---

## 1. The 4-Stage Interview Funnel

```
1. Screening Call (30m) ──► 2. Topgrading Interview (60m) ──► 3. Technical / Work Test (60m) ──► 4. Reference Checks
   Cultural filter &            Chronological career walkthrough  Hands-on realistic challenge      Verification of claims
   compensation check           Pivots, successes, mistakes       (Pair programming or case)        with former managers
```

---

## 2. The Chronological Topgrading Framework

For each significant past position on the candidate's CV (starting from most recent):
1. *"What were you hired to do, and what were your key measurable outcomes?"*
2. *"What accomplishments are you most proud of in that role?"*
3. *"What were the low points, mistakes, or things that didn't go well?"*
4. *"Who was your manager, and when I call them for a reference, what will they say were your biggest strengths and areas for growth?"*
5. *"Why did you leave that position?"*

---

## 3. Red Flags & Dealbreakers in Startup Hiring

| Signal | What it Reveals | Risk Level |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| Blames former colleagues/bosses for failures | Low accountability; external locus of control | **Immediate Disqualifier** |
| Speaks only in vague generalities ("we shipped") | Cannot articulate their specific contribution | High Risk |
| Unwilling to write code or test live during interview | Theoretical architect; lacks hands-on velocity | High Risk |
| Uncomfortable with rapid change or pivoting priorities | Thrives only in rigid corporate bureaucracies | Disqualifier for Seed/A |
| Dismissive of regulatory compliance, tests, or security | Cuts corners under pressure; introduces catastrophic liability | **Immediate Disqualifier** |

---

## 4. Reference Check Script (Torc Question)

*Call former direct managers, not just friends.*
- *"In what context did you work together?"*
- *"On a scale of 1 to 10, how would you rate their performance compared to peers?"*
- *"What are the specific reasons they are not a 10?"*
- *"If you had the opportunity, would you rehire them tomorrow without hesitation?"*
