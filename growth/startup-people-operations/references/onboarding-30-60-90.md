# 30-60-90 Day Startup Onboarding & Performance Framework

A structured onboarding plan ensures new hires reach full productivity rapidly, understand core company operating rhythms, and have clear alignment on expectations.

---

## 1. The 30-60-90 Day Milestones

### Days 1–30: Absorb & Ship Small
- **Goal**: Understand the codebase, architecture, customers, and team rhythm. Ship the first production commit in Week 1.
- **Milestones**:
  - Complete local dev environment setup on Day 1.
  - Read all core workspace rules, architecture docs, and skill standards.
  - Review 5 recent customer bug reports and 3 recorded sales/demo calls.
  - Ship at least 2 small bug fixes or minor features with full test coverage before Day 14.
  - Conduct 1:1 get-to-know sessions with every core team member.

### Days 31–60: Independent Execution & Ownership
- **Goal**: Take full ownership of a functional feature area or business objective.
- **Milestones**:
  - Lead design and implementation of a major milestone deliverable.
  - Participate in code reviews and PR feedback loops.
  - Identify at least one technical or process bottleneck and propose an optimization.
  - Day 60 Calibration Check-in: Formal review of early outcomes against Role Scorecard.

### Days 61–90: Full Velocity & Optimization
- **Goal**: Operating autonomously at full capability; driving strategic impact.
- **Milestones**:
  - Deliver on the first quarterly outcome defined in the Role Scorecard.
  - Proactively identify cross-functional risks or growth opportunities.
  - Full Day 90 Performance Review: Evaluation of cultural alignment, velocity, and probation sign-off.

---

## 2. Weekly 1:1 Operating Cadence

Hold a recurring 30-minute weekly 1:1 with direct reports:
- **10 Minutes (Employee's Agenda)**: What is on their mind? What went well this week?
- **10 Minutes (Manager's Agenda)**: Review progress against Scorecard outcomes and upcoming priorities.
- **10 Minutes (Blockers & Growth)**: Where are they stuck? What feedback do they have for the manager/company? What feedback does the manager have for them?

---

## 3. Early Warning Indicators & Fast Remediation

- If a new hire fails to ship code/work by Day 14, diagnose root cause immediately (environment issue, lack of context, or competence mismatch).
- If after 45 days there is a verified gap in core competency or accountability, address it directly with candid feedback.
- *Rule: Never let underperformance linger past 60 days without an explicit remediation plan or probation separation.*
