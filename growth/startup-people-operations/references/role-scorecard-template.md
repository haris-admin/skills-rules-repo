# Startup Role Scorecard Template (The "Who" Methodology)

Based on Geoff Smart and Randy Street's *Who: The A Method for Hiring*, a **Scorecard** defines what success actually looks like in a role *before* interviewing candidates. It replaces vague job descriptions with explicit measurable business outcomes.

---

## Role Scorecard: [Position Title]

- **Role Title**: (e.g. Founding Full-Stack AI Engineer, Head of B2B Compliance Sales)
- **Hiring Manager**: (Founder / CEO / Head of Eng)
- **Target Start Date**: (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Compensation Band**: Base Salary + Equity ESOP percentage range.

---

## 1. Role Mission
*A concise, executive summary of the core purpose of the role (1–2 sentences).*
> Example: *"Architect and ship resilient, multi-agent AI execution pipelines and B2B SaaS interfaces for AMLHive, driving customer onboarding latency under 60 seconds with zero downtime."*

---

## 2. Key Measurable Outcomes (The "What")
*3 to 5 objective, measurable milestones the person must achieve within their first 12 months.*

1. **Outcome 1**: (e.g., Deliver AMLHive v2.0 ASIC search API integration by Month 3 with $\ge 99.9\%$ uptime).
2. **Outcome 2**: (e.g., Reduce customer due diligence onboarding latency from 45 minutes to $< 60$ seconds by Month 6).
3. **Outcome 3**: (e.g., Implement and pass all AUSTRAC technical compliance audit requirements with zero high-severity findings).
4. **Outcome 4**: (e.g., Maintain test coverage $\ge 85\%$ and automated CI verification with zero manual deployment steps).

---

## 3. Core Competencies (The "How")
*Behavioral and technical traits required to achieve the outcomes.*

| Competency | Behavioral Definition | Rating Scale (1–5) |
|:---|:---|:---:|
| **Technical Excellence** | Writes modular, readable, well-tested code (React, Node, Python, Postgres). Solves root causes rather than patching symptoms. | Target $\ge 4$ |
| **High Autonomy** | Self-directed; operates effectively in fast-paced ambiguity with minimal supervision; unblocks self. | Target $\ge 4$ |
| **Integrity & Rigor** | Never cuts corners on security, secrets, or regulatory compliance rules. Owns mistakes openly. | Target $\ge 5$ |
| **Velocity & Tempo** | Ships small, verifiable increments daily. Prefers fast feedback loops over monolithic releases. | Target $\ge 4$ |
| **Collaborative Communication** | Writes concise PR descriptions, structured handoff notes, and communicates blockers proactively. | Target $\ge 4$ |
