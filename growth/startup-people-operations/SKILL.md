# Startup People Operations & Talent Architecture

A systematic human resources and talent framework tailored for early-stage and high-growth startups, incorporating Geoff Smart's **Scorecard Methodology**, structured behavioral interviewing, and disciplined **30-60-90 day onboarding**.

## When to Use

- **Hiring & Role Definition**: When opening a new role and defining explicit, measurable business outcomes rather than vague laundry lists of requirements.
- **Candidate Evaluation & Interviewing**: When running structured, bias-free candidate interviews, evaluating technical work tests, and checking managerial references.
- **New Hire Onboarding**: When ramping up new engineers, product managers, or commercial operators from Day 1 to full autonomous productivity in 90 days.
- **Team Performance & Retention**: When establishing weekly 1:1 meeting cadences, providing radical candor feedback, and addressing underperformance early.

## The 4 Pillars of Startup People Operations

```
  1. DEFINE (Scorecards)       2. SELECT (Structured Rubrics)
  ┌───────────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────────┐
  │   Role Mission        │    │ Topgrading Interview  │
  │   Measurable Outcomes │───►│ Hands-on Work Test    │
  │   Core Competencies   │    │ Reference Verification│
  └───────────────────────┘    └───────────┬───────────┘
                                           │
                                           ▼
  4. RETAIN (1:1 & Feedback)   3. ONBOARD (30-60-90 Days)
  ┌───────────────────────┐    ┌───────────────────────┐
  │   Weekly 1:1 Cadence  │    │ Days 1-30: Absorb     │
  │   Continuous Feedback │◄───│ Days 31-60: Own       │
  │   ESOP / Incentive    │    │ Days 61-90: Velocity  │
  └───────────────────────┘    └───────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Role Scorecards (The "Who" Method)
- Replace generic job descriptions with a 3-part Scorecard:
  1. **Role Mission**: Concise purpose statement.
  2. **Outcomes**: 3 to 5 quantitative results to be achieved in Year 1.
  3. **Competencies**: Observable behavioral and technical standards.

### 2. Structured Interviewing
- Run chronological Topgrading interviews across past career decisions, successes, low points, and former manager evaluations.
- Use realistic work tests (e.g. live code walkthrough or architecture challenge) over abstract trivia.

### 3. Rapid 30-60-90 Day Ramp-Up
- **Days 1–30 (Absorb)**: Ship code to production in Week 1; understand context; meet all team members.
- **Days 31–60 (Own)**: Take full ownership of an active feature area or business milestone.
- **Days 61–90 (Velocity)**: Operating at full speed; deliver first major quarterly outcome.

### 4. Continuous Feedback & 1:1 Rhythms
- Weekly 30-minute 1:1s split between Employee agenda, Manager agenda, and Blockers/Growth.
- Surface performance mismatches early; never let issues linger past 60 days.
