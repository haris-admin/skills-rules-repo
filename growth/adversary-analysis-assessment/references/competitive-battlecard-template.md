# Competitive Battlecard & Adversary Matrix Template

Use this template to equip sales, product, and strategy teams with concrete counter-arguments against direct competitors, legacy incumbents, and the status quo.

---

## Adversary Profile: [Adversary Name / Status Quo]

### 1. Executive Snapshot
- **Adversary Category**: Direct Competitor / Legacy Incumbent / Inertia & Spreadsheets
- **Target Audience**: Who buys from them today?
- **Pricing Model**: How do they charge? (Per seat, transaction percentage, hourly billing, opaque fees)

### 2. Strengths & Perceived Value
- What do customers praise about them?
- Where do they have enterprise credibility or brand recognition?

### 3. Structural Vulnerabilities & Traps
- **Economic Vulnerability**: Are they trapped by high margins or bloated headcount?
- **UX / Product Complexity**: Is their software dated, clunky, or hostile to neurodivergent/non-technical users?
- **Speed to Value**: Does onboarding take months of custom integration?

### 4. Asymmetric Attack Vectors ("How We Win")
| Buyer Objection | What the Competitor Claims | Our Winning Counter-Narrative |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Competitor X is the established industry leader."* | *"Nobody ever got fired for buying [Competitor]."* | *"Legacy systems were built 15 years ago for manual enterprise audits. Our platform automates the entire loop in 3 clicks at 80% lower cost."* |
| *"We already use spreadsheets and manual emails."* | *"It's free and everyone knows how to use it."* | *"Manual workflows leak an average of 14 hours per week and leave zero verifiable audit trail for regulatory deadlines."* |

### 5. The "Kill Shot" Question
Ask a prospective buyer a question that exposes the competitor's fundamental architectural flaw:
- *Example for AML Compliance*: "When AUSTRAC audits your entity, how many days will it take you to compile your historical PEP search logs across all clients?"
- *Example for Merchant Disputes*: "How many $35 chargeback penalties did your bank bill you last quarter for disputes that were actually friendly fraud?"
